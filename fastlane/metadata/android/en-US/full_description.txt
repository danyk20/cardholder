Cardholder keeps your bank cards, ID cards and loyalty cards in one place on your phone – encrypted, offline and free of ads and tracking.

<b>Loyalty cards at the checkout</b>
• Every loyalty card shows its barcode or QR code right in the list
• Tap it to show the code full screen at maximum brightness, ready for the scanner
• Scan the code from your plastic card with the camera – or type it in
• Supports QR, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC, Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, ITF, Codabar, PDF417, Aztec and Data Matrix

<b>Bank cards</b>
• Store card number, expiry date, card holder and CVV
• Fill in the details by holding a contactless card to your phone (NFC)
• The CVV is always hidden and only shown after fingerprint, face or screen lock
• Card network detected automatically

<b>ID cards</b>
• Identity cards, passports, driving licences and more
• Issuing country, document number and expiry date

<b>Photos of both sides</b>
• Optionally scan the front and back of any card with automatic edge detection
• Photos are encrypted and never leave your device

<b>Security and privacy</b>
• Everything is encrypted with keys kept in your phone's secure hardware
• Lock any card so that it can only be opened with fingerprint, face or screen lock
• Screenshots are blocked on screens with sensitive details
• Copied numbers are removed from the clipboard after a minute
• No account, no ads, no analytics – your cards never leave your device
• The app only goes online when you choose to download an official shop or bank logo

<b>Organised your way</b>
• Search and filter by card type
• Sort by name, date added or your own custom order
• Colours and logos for known shops and banks, or upload your own

<b>Encrypted backup</b>
• Export all cards to a single password-protected file and restore it on a new phone

Cardholder is open source: https://github.com/danyk20/cardholder
